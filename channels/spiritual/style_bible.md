# Style bible: Spiritual (Gita ka Gyaan)

CHANNEL: Gita ka Gyaan (working name)
NICHE: spiritual
VIEWER: Hindi-speaking 18-40 year olds who believe in God and are stuck on a real problem: anger, breakups, career confusion, family pressure, comparison, overthinking. They want guidance that feels both sacred and usable today.
PROMISE: Every video turns one Gita teaching into one thing you can do today.
VOICE: Calm, warm, unhurried elder-sibling tone. Respectful "aap" form. Hinglish captions, Hindi (Devanagari) for the TTS so pronunciation stays natural. No slang.
SIGNATURE: Open on Krishna, close on Krishna, so the video loops cleanly. Cite chapter.verse as on-screen text in the first beat.
STRUCTURE: 25-40 seconds, 4-6 beats: hook (the problem or a bold claim) -> what the verse says -> what it means today -> one practical action -> a closing line that reframes the viewer.
HOOK MIX: relatable scene 30%, question the viewer already has 25%, direct claim 20%, specific number 15%, stakes 10%.

ALWAYS
- Quote or paraphrase a real verse and cite it correctly, because devotional audiences check and a wrong citation destroys trust.
- Give one concrete action the viewer can do today, because that's what gets saves and shares.
- Keep each voiceover line speakable in one breath, because TTS reads at a steady pace.
- Show deities with reverence: serene, radiant, dignified.

NEVER
- Invent verses or attribute made-up quotes to Krishna or the Gita.
- Show deities in mocking, sexualized, violent or political contexts.
- Promise miracles, money, cures or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use scripture to answer a mental-health crisis; point to real help instead.
- Repeat an angle, verse+problem pair or opening line listed in PAST SCRIPTS.

VISUAL STYLE: reverent devotional Indian painting, soft golden divine light, rich saffron, blue and gold palette, serene, vertical 9:16, no text in images. Krishna descriptor (keep identical every time): "Lord Krishna, blue-skinned, peacock feather crown, yellow silk dhoti, gentle knowing smile, golden halo".
SOURCES: Bhagavad Gita (chapter.verse), standard translations. Paraphrase rather than reproduce long modern translations.
BANNED PHRASES: "Namaskar doston", "aaj ki video mein", "like share subscribe", "kya aap jaante hain" as an opener more than once a week.
PAST SCRIPTS: generated at run time from jobs/done, jobs/drafts and jobs/queue (titles + angle_tags).
